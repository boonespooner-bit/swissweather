The Bernese Oberland high passes remain snow-covered in mid-June. The Sefinenfurgga (8,570 ft) and Hohtürli (9,114 ft) crossings will likely require microspikes. This page refreshes weather data every 12 hours. Check daily from June 12 when reliable forecasts open up for your hike dates.

Daily Update Routine

What to check, and when.

A field-tested cadence for keeping ahead of conditions. Schedule these check-ins on your calendar now.

June 12
First reliable forecast. MeteoSwiss begins showing day-by-day forecasts for June 17–21. Check meteoswiss.admin.ch for Mürren, Kandersteg, Grindelwald.
June 12
Pull SLF snow depth map. Visit slf.ch — look at snow depth at 8,200 ft and 9,200 ft in the Bernese Oberland region.
June 13
Check Bergfex webcams — bergfex.com/jungfrau-grindelwald-wengen — to visually confirm snow line position.
June 14
Call Rotstockhütte: +41 33 855 24 64. Ask Simon Furrer about Sefinenfurgga conditions, snow depth on pass, and whether wire ropes are clear.
June 14
Call Gspaltenhornhütte: +41 33 676 16 29. Confirm the hut is open and ask about route conditions.
June 14
Call Blüemlisalphütte: +41 33 676 14 37. Confirm seasonal bridges are installed and the Hohtürli approach is passable.
June 15
Pull mountain-forecast.com for Schilthorn, Gspaltenhorn, and Blüemlisalphorn at multiple elevations.
June 16
Final go/no-go decision. Check 72-hour outlook on MeteoSwiss. If a major storm is forecast for Day 3 or Day 5, consider alternate routing.
Each AM
Ask the hut warden before leaving each morning. They get fresh forecasts and know the microclimates better than any app.
Each AM
Look at the sky. If clouds are building over the high peaks at 09:00, expect afternoon thunderstorms. Adjust your timing on exposed sections.
